ABOUT

AI research with real-world impact

Dr. Fahmid Al Farid is a faculty lecturer at BSBI in Berlin, where he teaches and conducts research in computer vision and artificial intelligence. His academic journey includes postdoctoral research at Multimedia University, Malaysia, and research positions at the University of Ulsan and Sungkyunkwan University in South Korea.

He holds a PhD in Information Technology from Multimedia University, an MSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Ulsan, and a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Chittagong. His work emphasizes practical, interpretable and efficient AI systems.
