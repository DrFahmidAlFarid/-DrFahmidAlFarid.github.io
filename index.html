ABOUT

AI research with real-world impact

Dr. Fahmid Al Farid is a Faculty Lecturer (Assistant Professor) at BSBI in Berlin. His professional experience spans Germany, Malaysia, South Korea and Bangladesh, including postdoctoral research at Multimedia University and research positions at Sungkyunkwan University and the University of Ulsan.

He completed his PhD at Multimedia University in 2023 with a specialization in Computer Vision & Artificial Intelligence, an MS in Computer Engineering at the University of Ulsan, and a BS in Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Chittagong. His research emphasizes practical, interpretable and efficient AI systems.
